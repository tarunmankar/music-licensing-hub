About the Creator

The Vision Behind Tarun Music

Tarun Mankar is an AI Content Developer, Generative AI Expert, and Cinematic Music Composer based in India. He founded Tarun Music with a singular mission: to democratize access to high-quality, legally safe background music for content creators, filmmakers, and media brands across the globe.

With deep expertise in generative AI and classical orchestration, Tarun bridges the gap between cutting-edge technology and timeless musical storytelling. Every track in the Tarun Music catalog is crafted to meet cinematic industry standards — from the emotional subtlety of ambient underscores to the explosive power of action trailer music.

As an official creator on the ElevenLabs Creator Marketplace — the world's leading audio AI platform valued at $11 billion and backed by Sequoia Capital and a16z — Tarun's music comes with the highest commercial licensing standards available. Every track is 100% monetization safe, with zero YouTube Content ID claims and perpetual worldwide rights.

Beyond music, Tarun is a thought leader in AI content development, building generative workflows, AI creative pipelines, and digital media systems. His portfolio spans AI tools, creative automation, and full-stack content development.
